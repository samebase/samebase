## Quick Take

- Front, Intercom, Mixpanel, and Buffer are the most useful references for
  structure and pacing.
- Airbnb is useful historically, but it is more a canonical startup example than
  a strong visual reference for Noter.
- Some of the older examples above are public archive copies rather than current
  official company pages.
