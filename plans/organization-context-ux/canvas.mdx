<Annotation id="all-vaults-boundary-note" title="Global view" targetId="all-vaults-desktop" placement="bottom">

Organization sections replace personal folders. The page combines work without flattening its owner.

</Annotation>

<Annotation id="organization-header-note" title="Route context" targetId="organization-providers-desktop" placement="bottom">

The owner in the URL is also the owner in the header and navigation links. The page has no local
owner dropdown.

</Annotation>

<Annotation id="mobile-context-note" title="Small-screen invariant" targetId="organization-vaults-mobile" placement="right">

The owner control stays in the main pane. It does not depend on the navigation pane being open.

</Annotation>

<Annotation id="switcher-scope-note" title="Only required choices" targetId="organization-switcher-popover" placement="right">

The menu switches context. Account settings and sign-out stay outside it.

</Annotation>
